Luca Autopost

Luca Autopost is a private publishing tool used only by Luca, an online fitness coach (Instagram and TikTok: @luc_asthetic_). It publishes short videos that Luca has produced and approved to his own Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts.

How it works: Luca reviews each finished video and its caption, then approves it. The tool uploads the approved video to his own accounts through the official Instagram API, the TikTok Content Posting API and the YouTube Data API. Before every TikTok post Luca sees his account name, picks the privacy setting and the interaction settings, and confirms the post.

The tool is not offered to other users and does not collect data from other people.

YouTube
