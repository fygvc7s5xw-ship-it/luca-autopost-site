Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Luca Autopost ("the app") is a private tool operated by Luca (@luc_asthetic_) to publish his own videos to his own social media accounts.

Data the app accesses

  • Basic account information of the connected account (account ID, display name, avatar), to show which account a post goes to.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to the connected Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens and account information are stored only on the operator's own computer, in the macOS Keychain. Videos and captions are created by the operator. Videos may be held for up to one hour in private cloud storage while a platform downloads them, and are deleted afterwards.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. Data is sent only to the platform the video is published to.

Other users

The app is used only by its operator and does not collect personal data from other people.

Deleting data

The operator can revoke access at any time in the Instagram, TikTok or Google account settings, which also makes stored tokens useless. To request deletion of any data, email ladjyn.fitness@gmail.com.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channel. It requests only the scopes needed to upload a video (youtube.upload) and to read the connected channel's name (youtube.readonly). It does not read, store or share any other YouTube data.

  • By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).
  • You can revoke the app's access to your Google and YouTube data at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and deletion

The only stored YouTube data is the OAuth refresh token, kept in the operator's macOS Keychain. Revoking access (link above) makes it unusable; it is deleted from the Keychain on request or when access is revoked. Uploaded videos stay on YouTube under the channel owner's control. Deletion requests: ladjyn.fitness@gmail.com, answered within 7 days.
